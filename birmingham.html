Registered vs attended

Who turned up, and what we can tell about them: when they registered, how they arrived, which brand and page sent them. Counts only, no names.

How to read this. “Registered” is one ticket per person (repeat bookings left out, 107 of them). “Attended” means the ticket was scanned at the door. Anyone let in without a scan is not counted, so real turnout is somewhat higher than shown. Scorp Staff (22 registered) were not scanned. These are fixed totals read from the Birmingham scanner and master sheets after the event; the filters on the main page do not change them.
